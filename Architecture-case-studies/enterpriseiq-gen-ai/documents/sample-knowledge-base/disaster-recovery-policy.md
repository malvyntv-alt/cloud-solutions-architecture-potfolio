# ExampleCorp — Disaster Recovery Policy (SYNTHETIC DEMO)

This document is fictional and intended solely for an AWS portfolio demonstration.

## Critical databases
For this fictional organization, critical databases target a recovery time objective (RTO) of 60 minutes and a recovery point objective (RPO) of 15 minutes. Recovery procedures are rehearsed quarterly.

## Responsibilities
The platform team owns backups and infrastructure recovery; application owners validate service readiness after failover.

## Evidence
Every recovery rehearsal records a timestamp, the measured RTO/RPO and corrective actions.
